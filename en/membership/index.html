Membership

The Gaspé Cancer Foundation is a community-based organization dedicated to supporting people affected by cancer in our region. By becoming a member, you directly contribute to our ability to offer essential services and financial assistance to patients undergoing treatment.

We offer one class of membership, for individuals or couples:

$15Individual / year
$25Couple / year

Membership benefits

  • Eligibility for travel assistance for your treatments;
  • Regular updates on our programs and initiatives;
  • Voting rights at the Annual General Meeting and any special members’ meeting.

Membership is valid from April 1 to March 31 of the following year. A membership not renewed by the last day of February lapses.
