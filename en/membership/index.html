Become a Member

Membership gives you access to the Foundation’s financial aid and a vote at the annual general meeting.

Individual

$15 / year

One person.

Couple

$25 / year

Two people at the same address.

Join or renew online · Online soon

What membership includes

  • Eligibility for financial aid for treatment-related travel;
  • News from the Foundation;
  • A vote at the annual general meeting.

Membership period

Membership runs from January 1 to December 31. Renewal is due on January 1 each year. A membership not renewed by the last day of February lapses.

Other ways to join

By cheque payable to the Gaspé Cancer Foundation, mailed to Gaspé Cancer Foundation, P.O. Box 6078, Gaspé, Quebec G4X 2R6, with your name, address, phone and email — or in person at the office on Fridays from 1 p.m. to 3 p.m.
