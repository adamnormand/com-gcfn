Our story

The Gaspé Cancer Foundation grew out of Noreen Hackett’s dream. At a women’s symposium she learned about the Linda LeMore-Brown Foundation, created to meet the financial needs of cancer patients in the Bonaventure district. She saw the same need in the Gaspé area.

The Foundation was established in July 2003 and registered as a charity that same year. It is run 100% by volunteers and serves residents from Grande-Vallée to Coin-du-Banc (Corner-of-the-Beach), including Murdochville.

It provides financial aid to people who must travel to Chandler, Sainte-Anne-des-Monts, Rimouski, Rivière-du-Loup, Quebec City, Montreal or Sherbrooke for treatment.

The Foundation is not affiliated with the Association du cancer de l’Est du Québec or the Canadian Cancer Society.

What cancer cannot do

We give our members resources, inspiration and the strength to face cancer. Because as powerful as cancer is, it has its limits:

  • It cannot cripple love
  • It cannot shatter hope
  • It cannot corrode faith
  • It cannot destroy peace
  • It cannot kill friendship
  • It cannot suppress memories
  • It cannot silence courage
  • It cannot invade the soul
  • It cannot steal eternal life
  • It cannot conquer the spirit

Every gift keeps someone on the road to care.
