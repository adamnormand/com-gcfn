The story of our founding

Our History

The Gaspé Cancer Foundation grew out of Noreen Hackett’s dream after she attended a women’s symposium where she learned of the Linda LeMore-Brown Foundation, formed to help meet the financial needs of cancer patients in the Bonaventure district. Noreen saw the same need in the Gaspé area.

The Foundation was formed in July 2003 and registered as a charity at that time. It is a non-profit run entirely by volunteers, serving residents from Grande-Vallée to Coin-du-Banc (Corner-of-the-Beach), including Murdochville.

The Foundation acts as a bilingual information centre and provides financial aid to people who must travel to Chandler, Sainte-Anne-des-Monts, Rimouski, Rivière-du-Loup, Quebec City, Montreal or Sherbrooke for treatment.

We are not affiliated with the Association du cancer de l’Est du Québec or the Canadian Cancer Society.
