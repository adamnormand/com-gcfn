Resources

Out-of-region hospitals and lodging, and help getting services in English.

Interpretation and support services

If you are treated at a predominantly French-speaking hospital, such as in Rimouski, and need services in English, ask the health professional who referred you, or call the hospital or CASA (Committee for Anglophone Social Action) at 1-877-752-5995 in advance to arrange an interpreter or help with lodging.

The listings below are provided for convenience. Always confirm the address and availability with the facility before you travel.
