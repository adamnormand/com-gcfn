Three Ways to Give

You can give online by credit or debit card, or offline by cheque or cash. Read below for details.

One-Time Donation

A one-time donation provides immediate support to people facing cancer in the territory we serve. It helps cover travel, lodging and meals for patients during a time of great need.

Donate a Lump Sum · Online soon

Recurring Donation

Become a sustaining partner in the fight against cancer. A monthly, quarterly or yearly gift gives the Foundation reliable funding, so we can plan ahead and offer consistent support. Stop at any time.

Donate a Recurring Amount · Online soon

In Memoriam Donation

Honour the memory of a friend or loved one lost to cancer. We let the family know of your gift, without the amount, and you receive an income tax receipt.

Donate In Memoriam · Online soon
Donate Securely, Support Directly

Donate Online

Online gifts are processed by Zeffy, a free platform for charities. The Foundation receives 100% of your gift: no platform or transaction fees.

At checkout, Zeffy suggests an optional contribution to its own operations. It is voluntary and you can set it to $0.

Your official tax receipt is emailed as soon as payment is confirmed. Monthly donors receive one cumulative receipt in January.

Other Methods to Give

Offline Payments

If you prefer not to give online, follow the steps below.

Download and Print the Form

You may mail a donation without a form, except for an in-memoriam gift or if you want an income tax receipt. In that case, include a note with your name and address, or the form below.

  • In Memoriam Donation Form (PDF)

Prepare Your Payment

We accept cheques and cash. Cheques are payable to the Gaspé Cancer Foundation. For recurring donations, post-dated cheques are recommended.

Send or Deliver Payment
