Why we use Zeffy

Online gifts are processed by Zeffy, a free platform for non-profits: the Foundation receives 100% of your gift, with no platform or transaction fees. At checkout, Zeffy suggests an optional contribution to its own operations — you can set it to $0.

Your official tax receipt is emailed automatically. Monthly donors receive one cumulative receipt in January.
