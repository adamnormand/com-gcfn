Request Assistance

Financial aid for members who must travel outside the region for cancer treatment.

Eligibility

Only members of the Foundation can request assistance. Requests must be supported by a health professional who referred you to a facility outside the region.

How to apply

  1. Check your membership. It must be in good standing for the current year.
  2. Download the referral form and print it.
  3. Have your physician complete the first part (referral) and the treating facility complete the second part (proof of attendance).
  4. Send us the form by mail, in person at the office on Fridays.
Download the referral form (PDF)

Questions about your request? Call one of our volunteers — their numbers are on the Contact Us page.
