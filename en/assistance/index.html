Request Assistance

Eligibility

Only members of the Gaspé Cancer Foundation can request assistance. Membership must be in good standing for the current year.

Referral

To make a claim, a member must have been referred to a facility outside the region by a health professional.

Download and print our referral form. Have your doctor complete the first part and the treating facility complete the second, then mail it to Gaspé Cancer Foundation, P.O. Box 6078, Gaspé, Quebec G4X 2R6. Forms are not accepted by email.

Download Referral Form

Questions about your request? Call one of our volunteers. Their numbers are on the Contact Us page.

An all-volunteer registered charity providing financial aid to people with cancer who must travel outside the region for treatment.
